# English Quest · Mission 01 homework

Three separate versions, with separate saved answers:

- **Movers:** `index.html`. One job, two qualities with two because reasons, two skills, original Tiko introduction video and five-gap recap video.
- **Starters:** `starters/index.html`. Six short sections with Russian instructions, about 10–15 minutes. Added repetition of the actual Mission 1 letters A/B/I/K/O/T, four letter-listening choices and a shuffled T-I-K-O name builder. The school-word game uses pencil, pen, book, ruler and eraser with the approved unlabelled Mission 1 illustrations; tap a word to listen/repeat, then place it under a picture. The original name, kind/funny, draw/jump/swim, two picture listening questions and the three-line spoken personal card remain. Only the personal name is typed. No job, age, portrait, diary or long videos.
- **Starters Plus:** `bridge/index.html`. The middle step: name and age, one of three jobs, one of four personal traits, one job-appropriate skill, three sentences assembled from shuffled word tiles, three picture listening questions, and a final spoken introduction. Pronunciation and listening instructions ask pupils to repeat aloud. After the personal sentences are checked, a 38-second recorded model opens with six lines and real pauses for repetition. Pupils then read their own five-sentence pass and try to tell it with less support. Russian is used for task explanations and the final self-confirmation; the crew pass and learning language are English. The same save key `english-quest-homework01-bridge-v1` retains previous answers and spoken confirmations.

In Movers the learner draws a portrait or uses their initials on a physical ID-card illustration, then types an introduction on a diary page. Both versions have local pronunciation buttons. Listening feedback supports correction and replay. The teacher assesses language quality and speaking; the page checks required parts and listening choices only.

Speaking checkboxes are learner confirmations, not automatic speech assessment. The simple Starters version has its own v2 save key; earlier Starters work and Movers work remain stored separately. Restart clears only the simple version.

The new Starters repetition saves separately under `english-quest-homework01-starters-review-v1`; the existing v2 personal-card answers, audio checks and shuffled choices are retained. Letter and word banks stay in their saved order during checking/retry. School pictures initially have no target captions; only the pupil's placed words appear below them. Completion now includes both repetition games and the existing personal/listening tasks. Confirmed Start again resets both parts of Starters only. Art and speech reuse `static/starters-quest/supplies-v1.png` and the actual Mission 1 audio, prepared by `scripts/build_mission01_starters_review.py`. There is no browser speech service dependency.

Open the desired HTML file while keeping the folder structure. Shared assets, scripts and styles must remain in the parent Mission 01 folder. No accounts, external fonts, build step, speech service or live connection are needed once files have been downloaded. Responses and drawing stay in localStorage in the same browser/device.

Videos were transcoded from the approved source videos without altering their content. The excluded original `Tiko's HW.MP3` is not included.
